The slotsmith date picker limits what can be picked in two ways: `minDate` and `maxDate` set the earliest and the latest date, and `disabledDates` blocks single days such as weekends or booked nights. Blocked days stay focusable, but cannot be picked.

The appointment can be any weekday from today to two months ahead. Open it and move with the arrow keys: focus crosses the weekends, and stops at today.

## minDate and maxDate

[`minDate`](../../api/#prop-minDate) and [`maxDate`](../../api/#prop-maxDate) are `YYYY-MM-DD` strings, both inclusive. With them set:

- Days outside the bounds cannot be picked.
- The calendar does not go past the bounds' months: the previous and next buttons are disabled there.
- The arrow keys, <kbd>PgUp</kbd> and <kbd>PgDn</kbd> stop at the bounds.
- The year select lists the bounds' years only. Without bounds it lists ten years either side of the year on screen.

## disabledDates

[`disabledDates`](../../api/#prop-disabledDates) is a function that receives a date and returns `true` to block it. The demo blocks Saturdays and Sundays; a set of booked dates works the same way: `(date) => booked.has(date)`.

A blocked day, like a day outside the bounds, gets `aria-disabled="true"` and `data-disabled`, never the `disabled` attribute. It stays focusable, so the arrow keys cross it and a screen reader announces it. A click or <kbd>Enter</kbd> on it does nothing.

In range mode, `disabledDates` stops a blocked day from being picked as the start or the end. It does not stop a range from spanning blocked days. If a stay must not include a booked night, check the range in `onChange`.

## Disabling the whole control

[`disabled`](../../api/#prop-disabled) is separate from `disabledDates`: it turns off the whole picker, like the second one in the demo. The trigger gets `aria-disabled="true"`, leaves the tab order, does not open, and shows no clear button.
